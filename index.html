02 / WHY ME

I'm useful in the messy middle.

Where business pressure, product decisions, engineering constraints, and operator experience collide. That is the part I like making legible.

01BUSINESS ↔ PRODUCT

Business context stays attached to the build.

I keep the reason behind a request visible while the solution moves through design, implementation, edge cases, and iteration.

02PRODUCT ↔ ENGINEERING

I can go technical without turning the meeting into theatre.

I read logs, follow data behavior, question architecture trade-offs, and understand frontend or backend constraints well enough to make better product decisions.

03DELIVERY ↔ EXPERIENCE

UX is part of delivery quality, not decoration.

A feature is not finished when it exists. It should communicate state clearly, respond quickly, and fit the operator workflow without unnecessary friction.
