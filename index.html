About

I care about the invisible layer behind a good product:the workflow, decisions, constraints, and details that make it feel obvious.

My work sits between business needs and implementation. I map processes, challenge unnecessary complexity, coordinate delivery, and keep the product experience coherent from the first decision to the final interaction.

The goal is not to decorate complexity. It is to reduce it, expose what matters, and make the system feel calm even when the problem behind it is anything but.
